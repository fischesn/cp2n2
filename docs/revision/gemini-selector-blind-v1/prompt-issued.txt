FEASIBILITY RULES

  reset_penalty

reset_penalty is 1 exactly when:
  task reset_free is true AND resource stateful is true.
Otherwise it is 0.

  locality_penalty,
  cost_unknown,
  cost_or_0

locality_penalty:

cost_or_0:
