# AP1 required-evidence campaign v1 — 14 October 2026

This is a new software-only campaign, separate from all frozen R2–R5 archives.
Freeze this protocol, case expectations, environment and source bytes before
scored execution. Development runs are not confirmatory runs. Gemini cases
are withheld and neither supplied to the implementation nor used here.

## Authority and observations

Use official MCP in-memory sessions and the existing isolated E0 effect backend.
The host publishes a fixed preset requiring health, drift, information age and
calibration status (except the explicitly optional-drift control). Calibration
is a software fixture's `valid` state, not physical calibration. The host sets
a 0.25 result-confidence requirement in the stored task; agents cannot supply
these policy fields. Observe starts, persisted commits and delivered successes
separately. Backend effect records are checked independently of production
admission and validation. Faults belong to the trusted, isolated test binding.
Unlike the historical R3 fixture, this new fixture's preparation checks only
the fixed vector input, not telemetry. Its invocation produces effects even
with invalid telemetry if the gateway lets it through. Thus age/calibration
rejections cannot be credited to a provider-side preparation guard. The
fixture configuration and any preparation are retained in the archive.

Two repetitions of every ordinary case and each of two detector controls are
fixed in the runner's specification. Invalid required drift, information age
or calibration must produce no backend start or commit. Invalid post-execution
evidence must withhold successful delivery, but a legitimate prior commit is
not mislabeled as a forbidden effect. Fresh coherent required evidence and
optional absent drift must actually execute; denying everything fails.

Cached timestamped health observations retain their original observation and
validity times. An expired cached observation stays expired after republication.
The fresh-cache positive control must execute. This tests preservation of
age/expiry, not cryptographic replay resistance. The contradiction fixture
reports different health values in two representations of the same property;
it is not a general sensor-fusion or physical-truth experiment.

Output corruptions occur at the isolated adapter-return boundary after the
backend has logged its genuine commit. Preserve both the original and exposed
invocation. No expected outcome is adapted to an observed result. Detector
controls deliberately bypass the control plane or deny a valid request, and
are excluded from the ordinary-case forbidden-effect denominator.

## Verification and limits

Retain all MCP requests/responses, hash-chained audit events, host-owned policy,
contract snapshots, backend ledger/state, invocation corruption and final
cleanup state. The offline verifier recomputes counts, audit pairing, effect
hashes and the predeclared expectation matrix, and detects tampering.
Restore a faulted fixture only after its measured response, then reconcile
for cleanup; do not force lifecycle READY or silently repair scored data.

No hosted-model request, Docker service, physical device or living tissue is
used. A fresh but physically false trusted report can still pass. No durable
restart ownership, distributed consensus, generic sensor contradiction resolver
or physical-safety guarantee is established.
