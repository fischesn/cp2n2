## Contents and privacy

The ZIP contains all four byte-preserved confirmatory archives: AP1 required
evidence, AP2 structured provider failures and explicit recovery, AP3 timing
and CPU context, and the externally authored Gemini selector cases. Protocols,
inputs, prompts, approval/provenance records, raw observations, audit chains,
result reports and per-file manifests remain bound to their frozen sources.

Two AP2 development archives are separately labelled as unscored development
evidence, preserving the v1 negative classifier and the subsequent v2 check.
They are excluded from confirmatory denominators. The historical R4 offline
analysis is also labelled separately; it adds no new measurement and does not
rewrite earlier raw trials or their oracle.

Recursive structured-field and known-credential checks passed across 3,377
blobs and 79,394,986 expanded bytes. No `.env` is packaged, and no known
credential was found. The
supplement requires no financial-value redaction: synthetic cost and budget
fields are part of the experimental inputs and remain unchanged. These checks
are not a guarantee against every possible hidden secret in arbitrary binary
content.

The record also publishes the package and campaign verification JSONs,
metadata snapshot and SHA-256 sums. Documentation inside the ZIP is its frozen
prepublication checkpoint, not a silently rewritten current status report.

## Verification and limits

ZIP CRC and all 1,632 inventory payload hashes pass. All four offline campaign
verifiers pass using the extracted archived sources, not the live working
tree. The extracted payloads and private originals remain byte-identical.

Zenodo's file sizes and MD5 checksums match the local uploads. All five new
files, including the complete raw-evidence ZIP, were then downloaded without
authentication and match their complete local SHA-256 hashes and byte counts.
The [publication receipt](supplementary-zenodo-publication.json) and
[public download verification](supplementary-public-download-verification.json)
record these checks without credentials.

The actual version DOI is now integrated in the journal, neutral arXiv view
and reviewer response, with matching rebuilt PDF/source and Word/PDF packages.
All 88 output pages, source/PDF bindings, template fidelity and byte-identical
reviewer mirrors pass. Twenty-four paper/workflow tests and 36 supplementary
packaging/publication tests pass. The final artifact QA receipt is
`tmp/supplementary-publication-final-qa/verification.json` in the paper repository.
The source-hash check confirms that evaluated software and Gemini expectations
were not changed by publication or DOI integration.

Publication and verification add no scored observations, provider/model calls
or physical-substrate results. Gemini provides separate model authorship of
twelve synthetic cases, not independent human reproduction or 72 independent
workloads. Same-host automated verification is not independent co-author
reproduction. AP2 does not establish durable restart/competing-controller
guarantees, and no physical-safety or general replay-resistance claim follows
from this archive.
